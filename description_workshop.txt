[b]Off to Terraria, Meowster? Count me in![/b]
Bring a Palico from Monster Hunter along on your Terraria adventures.

[h2]Meet your Palico[/h2]

[b]A helping paw in combat.[/b] Give your Palico a weapon and fight side by side. Equip the mod's new support gear for extra help in combat. When you're hurt, it can bring you nearby Hearts, too.

[b]Help with the daily work.[/b] Your Palico can mine ore, chop and replant trees, tend herbs, fish, catch critters and collect items. It can also light up dark areas and point out nearby chests and Life Crystals.

[b]Make it your own.[/b] Choose a name, outfit color and dye. Your Palico chats with you and reacts with emotes. You can pet it, too.

[i]“I wasn't lost, Meowster! Just took the long way.”[/i]

[h2]Customize your Palico’s abilities[/h2]
The [b]Companion abilities[/b] settings offer three strength presets: choose [b]Support[/b] if you want to take the lead in combat, [b]Standard[/b] to fight side by side, or [b]Strong[/b] if you want to take it easy and let your Palico carry you. Standard is the default.

You can also fine-tune your Palico’s damage, health, defense, movement speed, ability to dodge attacks, work speed and fishing speed individually.

[h2]Equipment and inventory[/h2]
Equip your Palico and save multiple loadouts to switch between.

Your Palico has its own backpack for supplies and loot. Give it access to selected chests, and it can put items away alongside others of the same type and restock its supplies.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/main/docs/images/04-equipment-en-compact.png[/img]

[h2]Upgrades and the Palico Guide[/h2]
Gather materials to craft and upgrade your Palico’s gear. The in-game Palico Guide explains how to craft, use and upgrade equipment.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/main/docs/images/05-guide-en-compact.png[/img]

[h2]Bring your friends[/h2]
Play solo or in multiplayer, [b]with existing characters and worlds[/b]. Each player has a Palico with separate equipment, inventory and upgrades. The host or server administrator manages the shared Companion abilities settings.
