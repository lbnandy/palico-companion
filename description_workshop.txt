Bring a Monster Hunter-inspired Palico along for your Terraria adventures.

[h2]Your Palico companion[/h2]

[b]Fight together[/b]: Give your Palico a weapon and fight side by side. Support gear can heal you, shield you and clear debuffs; your Palico can also throw pot bombs or charge into a group carrying a Giga Barrel Bomb. When you're hurt, it can bring you nearby Hearts, too.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/main/docs/images/barrel-bomb.gif?v=emote1101[/img]

[b]Gather and explore[/b]: Your Palico can mine ore, chop and replant trees, tend herbs, fish, catch critters and collect items. It can also light up dark areas and point out nearby chests and Life Crystals.

[b]Make it yours[/b]: Choose a name, outfit color and dye. Your Palico chats with you and reacts with emotes. When things are quiet, you can pet it, feed it or even fly it as a kite.

[i]“Boss, that's not rain—it's slimes, meow!”[/i]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/main/docs/images/cart.gif[/img]

[h2]Customize your Palico's abilities[/h2]
The Companion abilities settings offer three strength presets: choose [b]Support[/b] if you want to take the lead in combat, [b]Standard[/b] to fight side by side, or [b]Strong[/b] if you want to take it easy and let your Palico carry you. Standard is the default.

You can also adjust damage, survivability, movement speed, dodging, targeting range, work speed and fishing speed individually. An optional boss health bonus adds a little more challenge when fighting together; it is off by default.

[h2]Equipment and inventory[/h2]
Equip your Palico and save multiple loadouts to switch between.

Your Palico has its own backpack for supplies and loot. Give it access to selected chests, and it can put items away alongside others of the same type and restock its supplies. Favorited items remain usable and stay in its backpack when you retrieve everything else.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/main/docs/images/04-equipment-en-compact.png[/img]

[h2]Equipment upgrades and the Palico Guide[/h2]
Gather materials to craft and upgrade your Palico's gear. The in-game Palico Guide explains how to craft, use and upgrade equipment.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/main/docs/images/05-guide-en-compact.png[/img]

[h2]Play with friends, too[/h2]
Supports single-player and multiplayer. [b]No new character or world required.[/b] Each player has their own Palico with separately saved equipment, inventory and progression. The host or server administrator manages ability settings for everyone.

[h2]Optional integrations[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2563309347]Magic Storage[/url]: let your Palico use your storage to put items away and restock its supplies.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2797518634]Quality of Terraria[/url]: its unlimited-ammo feature works for your Palico, too.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2824688072]Calamity[/url]: extra dialogue for its enemies and events.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2909886416]Thorium[/url]: supports Bard instruments, healing tools and throwing weapons. Let your Palico play music to provide buffs or use healing weapons to restore your health.
[/list]
None of these mods are required.
