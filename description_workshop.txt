[b]Off to Terraria, Meowster? Count me in![/b]
Bring a Palico from Monster Hunter along on your Terraria adventures.

[h2]Meet your Palico[/h2]

[b]A helping paw in combat.[/b] Give your Palico a weapon and fight side by side. Add support gear such as the Vigorwasp Cage or Shield for healing and protection.

[b]Help with the daily work.[/b] Your Palico can mine ore, chop and replant trees, tend herbs, fish and collect items. Underground, it can carry a torch and point out nearby chests and Life Crystals.

[b]Make it your own.[/b] Choose a name, outfit color and dye. Your Palico chats about what you encounter and reacts with emotes. You can pet it, too.

[i]“I wasn't lost, Meowster! Just took the long way.”[/i]

[h2]Equipment and inventory[/h2]
Mix and match weapons, support gear and work gear, then save loadouts to switch between them.
Your Palico has its own backpack for supplies and loot. Let it use selected chests, and it will put items away in chests that already contain them and restock its supplies automatically.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/main/docs/images/04-equipment-en-compact.png[/img]

[h2]Upgrades and the Palico Guide[/h2]
Unlock new gear as you progress, and improve your equipment with Improved and Masterwork Toolkits.
The in-game Palico Guide explains what each piece of gear does, how to craft it and how to give your Palico work orders. It also tracks upgrades and shows what you can craft next.
Automatic tasks and pickup preferences can be adjusted in the mod settings.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/main/docs/images/05-guide-en-compact.png[/img]

[h2]Bring your friends[/h2]
Play solo or in multiplayer. Each player gets their own Palico, with separate equipment, inventory and upgrades. [b]Works with existing characters and worlds—no fresh start needed.[/b]

If you use [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2563309347]Magic Storage[/url], let your Palico use your storage to put items away and restock its supplies. It also follows [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2797518634]Quality of Terraria[/url]'s rules for unlimited ammo and has dialogue for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2824688072]Calamity[/url] encounters. None of these mods are required.

Questions and suggestions are welcome in the comments or on [url=https://github.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/issues]GitHub[/url]. For bug reports, please include steps to reproduce the issue and your other enabled mods.
