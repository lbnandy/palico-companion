Bring a Monster Hunter-inspired Palico along for your Terraria adventures.

[h2]Your Palico companion[/h2]

[b]Fight together[/b]: Give your Palico a weapon and fight side by side. Support gear can heal you, shield you and clear debuffs; your Palico can also throw pot bombs or charge into a group carrying a Giga Barrel Bomb. When you're hurt, it can bring you nearby Hearts, too.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/main/docs/images/barrel-bomb.gif?v=barrel-prone1101[/img]

[b]Gather and explore[/b]: Your Palico can mine ore, chop and replant trees, tend herbs, fish, catch critters and collect items. It can also light up dark areas and point out nearby chests and Life Crystals.

[b]Make it yours[/b]: Choose a name, outfit color and dye. Your Palico chats with you and reacts with emotes. When things are quiet, you can pet it, feed it or even fly it as a kite.

[h2]Equipment and inventory[/h2]

Choose between two attribute modes:
[b]Player-stat inheritance[/b]: Use the owner's stats without preparing a separate set of armor and accessories.
[b]Independent equipment[/b]: Equip your Palico with its own armor and accessories to gain their stats and effects.

Switch modes in [b]Companion abilities[/b].

Main weapons and summon weapons have separate slots, so your Palico can fight alongside its minions or sentries. Save multiple loadouts and share favorited equipment with matching empty slots.

Your Palico carries supplies and loot in its own backpack and can use chests you allow to store items and restock supplies. Favorited items stay in its backpack when you retrieve everything else.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/main/docs/images/04-equipment-independent-en.png[/img]

[h2]Equipment upgrades and the Palico Guide[/h2]
Craft and upgrade your Palico's work and support gear with collected materials. The in-game Palico Guide covers recipes, uses and upgrades.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lbnandy/palico-companion/main/docs/images/05-guide-en-compact.png[/img]

[h2]Customize your Palico's abilities[/h2]
Both attribute modes support three strength presets in Companion abilities: choose [b]Support[/b] if you want to take the lead in combat, [b]Standard[/b] to fight side by side, or [b]Strong[/b] if you want to take it easy and let your Palico carry you. Standard is the default.

You can also adjust damage, survivability, movement speed, dodging, targeting range, work speed and fishing speed individually. An optional boss health bonus adds a little more challenge when fighting together; it is off by default.

[h2]Play with friends, too[/h2]
Supports single-player and multiplayer. [b]No new character or world required.[/b] Each player has their own Palico with separately saved equipment, inventory and progression. Each player chooses their own attribute mode; the host or server administrator manages shared settings such as strength multipliers.

[h2]Optional integrations[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2563309347]Magic Storage[/url]: let your Palico use your storage to put items away and restock its supplies.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2797518634]Quality of Terraria[/url]: its unlimited-ammo feature works for your Palico, too.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2824688072]Calamity[/url]: extra dialogue for its enemies and events.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2909886416]Thorium[/url]: supports Bard instruments, healing tools and throwing weapons. Let your Palico play music to provide buffs or use healing weapons to restore your health. You can also heal it with weapons that heal allies.
[/list]
None of these mods are required.
